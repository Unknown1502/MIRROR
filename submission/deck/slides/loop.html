Explore freely, commit exactly, verify always

1

Fork

Every reading the ERP’s own data allows. Unknown names resolve to nothing, never a guess.

2

Simulate

Against a frozen snapshot. Zero writes, deterministic, under a millisecond per future.

3

Clarify

Only the question that changes the outcome, with the reason in numbers.

4

Explore

“What if 900?”, “compare 500 and 900”, “as much as is safe”. Reality stays untouched.

5

Commit

Only on the user’s own “commit”, with a token bound to one version, its evidence and its simulation.

6

Verify

Read the whole world back. Anything but an exact match is “diverged”, with a rollback ready.

The loop. Steps one to four are free: nothing real changes, so the operator can explore by voice as much as they like. Step five is the only door to reality, and it needs the user's own word commit plus a token for the exact version they saw. Step six never trusts the system's report: it reads everything back.
