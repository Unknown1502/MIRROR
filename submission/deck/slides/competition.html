Where today's options stop

ApproachWhat it does wellWhat it misses
Voice agents with tool callingNatural conversation, fast actionActs on the model's reading
"Are you sure?" confirmationsA human in the loopNo consequences shown; people click yes
Prompt and output guardrailsBlocks unsafe textNever sees the effect on real data

MIRRORSimulates every reading, asks only what mattersCommits exactly one version, then verifies reality
Here is where the alternatives stop. Voice agents with tool calling are fast but act on whatever the model understood. Confirmation prompts put a person in the loop but show no consequences, so people learn to say yes. Guardrails check the text, never the effect on real data. Automation is reliable but cannot handle ambiguity or what-ifs. MIRROR combines the conversation of a voice agent with a simulated preview of every reading, a single signed commit, and a check that reality agrees.
