How MIRROR makes money

Seats

A monthly subscription per operator using the voice console, including the audit ledger.

Verified transactions

Usage pricing per committed and verified change, so cost follows the value delivered.

Connectors

Paid adapters for the systems customers already run, such as SAP, NetSuite and major WMS suites.

Private deployment

An annual licence to run it on premises, for regulated and security-sensitive operations.

Go to market: mid-size distributors first, through the integrators who already install their WMS and ERP.
