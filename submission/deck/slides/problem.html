Voice agents jump from language to action

“Move 700 XR-17 from Mumbai to the Pune site.”

There are two Pune sites. A typical agent guesses, or asks a generic “which one?”, and then writes to the inventory system. If it guessed wrong, nobody finds out until the stock is in the wrong place.

Guess

Confirm everything

“Are you sure?” on every step, until people say yes without reading.

Trust the reply
